01 / THE RABBIT HOLE

Monofoki, with my
own changes.

Monofuki is an unapologetic fork of Monofoki that I’ve fucked with for my own selfish purposes. It fixes the missing characters and terminal annoyances that bother me. If it works for you too, great. How you pronounce it is your business, not mine.

Upstream credit: Marvin Dostal’s Monofoki, based on Matthias Tellen’s Mononoki. I mainly use Terminal.app and iTerm2 on macOS. Your setup and taste may differ.

03 / BEYOND ASCII

The rest of
the terminal.

Nerd Font icons bring file types, folders and prompt symbols to the terminal. Monofuki Nerd Font pairs them with the letters and terminal graphics used in apps like Codex, oh-my-pi (omp), and Crush.

Both font variants include 415 added Unicode symbols and all 256 Braille patterns. Choose Monofuki Nerd Font for the Nerd Fonts 3.5.1 icons too. Your operating system can supply emoji and other characters through font fallback.

See the 415 additions and how font fallback works.

I wanted fewer missing symbols and gaps in the artwork I actually see. These samples show the cases I’ve worked on; they aren’t a promise of universal coverage.
