Crossbar help

Angle 0° is horizontal; positive rises right. X/Y locate its center in font units; positive Y moves up. Independent of digit shortening and shared by all four styles. Extreme positions may detach the dash. Default: −2°, 278 × 80 at (315, 235).

Accent behavior

Moves capitals and their top accents down together. Plain capitals, lowercase, below marks and Ľ’s side caron are excluded. Edit bodies and gaps in the character controls. Supported combining forms use the same outlines; unsupported forms show a missing-glyph cell. Tall marks can extend above the cell.

Spacing experiments

Spacing behavior

Downloads encode these fixed-advance ink shifts in the OpenType kern feature. Apps may disable it. No cell moves or changes width; no ligatures or ellipsis substitution. Above 100%, extra movement is conservatively limited to avoid adding collisions. Existing italic overlaps in the approved baseline are retained. The comparison always shows the configured experiments. Reading uses them only with strict mode off. ASCII previews and artwork always stay strict.

Technical notes & limitations

Capitals and tall lowercase letters shorten above y=520. Other lowercase letters and digits shorten above their baseline or ink bottom. Punctuation splits at its ink midpoint. Below-baseline ink has a separate tail control. Space has no outline.

These controls move Bézier points; they do not optically redraw the letters. Curves and stroke thickness can change. Top and tail shortening retain at least 75% of each band’s height. The y junction moves 0–150 units without reversing its vertical mapping, even at maximum shortening. Check the results at your intended size.

The y junction control raises both joins and tapers into the arms and tail. It leaves horizontal coordinates, top height and the deep tail unchanged relative to your other settings.

Q/y/j tail controls move the rounded ends inward without squeezing them. The g control compresses its lower loop or italic tail, tapering into the unchanged body. Cell advances stay at 575 units.

The 7 crossbar is a separate rounded shape in final font coordinates, shared by all four styles. Angle, end-to-end length, perpendicular thickness and center X/Y are independent of digit shortening. It never changes the numeral’s body or advance. Zeroing 7 removes it; restoring its default brings back your −2°, 278 × 80 dash at (315, 235). The original-contour overlay omits the dash.

The 81 supported accented capitals separate body and mark contours. Top marks follow the shortened body plus your gap setting; below marks follow its lower edge. Ľ’s side caron stays in place. “Follow base” links to the plain capital’s shortening. Whole-glyph lowering affects the 68 top-accent capitals. Marks never stretch; arbitrary stacked accents are unsupported. The shape preview draws vectors, so check compiled fonts in the playground and your terminal.

The alternative q, dotted zero and braces are drawn from Monofuki’s outlines. Originals remain available. Brace choices apply to both braces while keeping their separate shortening values.
