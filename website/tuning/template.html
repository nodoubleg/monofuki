Crossbar help

Angle 0° is horizontal; positive rises right. X/Y locate its center in font units; positive Y moves up. Independent of digit shortening and shared by all four styles. Extreme positions may detach the dash. Default: −2°, 278 × 80 at (341, 318).

Accents

Accent behavior

Whole-glyph lowering moves each above-accent capital and mark together. It excludes plain capitals, lowercase, below marks and Ľ’s side caron. Body and gap edits live with each character. Canonically equivalent combining forms use the same outline; unsupported combinations show a missing-glyph cell. Tall marks can extend above the cell.

Spacing behavior

Technical notes & limitations

Capitals and tall lowercase letters shorten above y=520. Short lowercase letters and numerals shorten above their baseline (or positive ink bottom). Punctuation uses its own ink midpoint, with separate upper- and lower-edge controls. Letters with ink below the baseline also have a tail control; tiny overshoots have correspondingly small limits. Space has no outline to adjust.

Top/tail-height and junction controls move coordinates vertically. Horizontal tail controls are separate and never scale the whole glyph. These are approximate Bézier control-point morphs, not optical redraws. Curves and stroke thickness can change, especially g/y tails, round capitals, digits and punctuation. The base top/tail-height controls retain at least 75% of each band height. The y junction range is now 0–150 units. Even combined with maximum top and tail-height adjustments, its vertical mapping remains monotonic (minimum slope above 0.10); it does not reverse vertically. This does not guarantee production-quality contours.

The y-only junction control translates the whole band from y=−20 through 110 upward, covering the inner junction at 63 or 83 and outer join at −10 in all four styles. A smooth taper leaves original points at/below −120 and at/above 400 unchanged by this control. The junction control preserves top height, x coordinates and deep-tail points relative to your other settings, while changing the nearby arms and tail transition. Inner and outer junctions move together; this is still an approximate contour morph.

Q/y/j truncation moves each rounded terminal inward without scaling the terminal itself; only the connecting tail span is shortened horizontally. The bowl, arms, stem and dot are excluded. The g control compresses only its descender horizontally: the lower loop in upright styles, or the returning tail in italic styles, with a tapered connection to the unchanged body. These local Bézier morphs preserve the original 575-unit advance and 1150-unit cell; the font download applies these same transformations.

The 7 crossbar is a separate rounded shape in final font coordinates, shared by all four styles. Angle, end-to-end length, perpendicular thickness and center X/Y are independent of digit shortening. It never changes the numeral’s body or advance. Zeroing 7 removes it; restoring its default brings back your −2°, 278 × 80 dash at (341, 318). Original-contour and 90% reference views omit the dash.

For short lowercase or numeral top adjustments, x-height or digit height changes intentionally. The 81 decomposable accented capitals have exact body/mark contour maps across all four styles. Shortening affects only the matched base body. Top accents follow its shortened top rigidly, plus your signed gap setting; below marks follow its lower overshoot. Ľ’s side caron stays in place. “Follow base” links body shortening to the current ASCII base settings; otherwise each accented capital is independent. Whole-glyph lowering translates the complete result and affects only the 68 top-accent capitals. Marks are never stretched. This embedded subset includes multi-contour marks, not arbitrary stacked Unicode accents. Tall accents may extend above a cell at zero lowering. The browser draws vectors rather than terminal-hinted fonts.

The q, dotted-zero and linked-brace alternatives are Monofuki-native experimental contours. Originals remain available. The q keeps its native bowl with a small rightward descender foot. Dotted zero preserves the outer body and removes the slash before adding the dot. Selecting either brace alternative applies to both braces, without changing their individual shortening values. The picker and preview render the selected outlines in all four styles.
