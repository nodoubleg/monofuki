06 / EMOJI

Why some symbols become emoji.

Text and emoji presentation

Some symbols have text and emoji forms. An invisible variation selector requests one: U+FE0E for text, U+FE0F for emoji. Without it, the character’s default and your app decide.

TEXT / FE0E☀︎ ☕︎ ♥︎U+2600 U+FE0E
EMOJI / FE0F☀️ ☕️ ♥️U+2600 U+FE0F

The text and emoji examples above use your browser’s font fallback.

Selectors only work for standardized pairs. On macOS, Apple Color Emoji can supply the emoji even when Monofuki is selected.

U+FFFD usually means text was decoded incorrectly. Changing fonts cannot recover characters lost during decoding.
